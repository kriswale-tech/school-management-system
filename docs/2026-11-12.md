## School app — settings

Alerts use toggle switches. The switch sits immediately in front of its label. Each row is separated, and the outstanding-fees threshold stays inside that row. Curriculum version adoption has its own switch, the same idea as the notice while the next term still needs action.

Under Settings → Messaging, the inner tab, heading, and save button are **Messages config**. Report-ready and debt-reminder help text points there. A guardian’s own Parent messages preference is unchanged.

## School app — curriculum adoption

Publishing a curriculum version does not rewrite existing schools. A school stays on the version it was provisioned from until an admin adopts.

With a current term open, adopt records the accepted version and leaves the term as it is. Opening the next term applies that version. Before any current term exists, adopt applies immediately.

Admins see three notices until the school accepts: the bell, an email to the school address and to active school-admin addresses, and a dashboard notice that can be dismissed for that version. The bell and the dashboard notice follow the curriculum alert switch. Manage classes shows the adoption banner.

A rename that has already been taught keeps the old name on the record and adds the new name going forward. Dropped items are retired. School-created subjects are left alone. Shared pre-school subject names are not listed as new when only another level changed.

## Platform admin — curriculum

The live version is cards with class chips and subject chips. Older published versions start collapsed. Removing a level or a class asks for confirmation. Cancel returns to the live list, and a saved draft stays as Draft in progress.

## Platform admin — overview

The home screen is three blocks, plus a Needs attention list that appears only when something is waiting.

- **Schools** — total, active, students, and staff, linking to the directory.
- **Messaging, last 30 days** — revenue, profit with the margin, cost (free and sponsored called out under it), cash collected, SMS segments, email credits, and failed sends. Right now: the Arkesel SMS wallet and how much of the email plan is used this month. A role that cannot see money gets usage, failures, and the wallet.
- **Curriculum** — the live version and how many schools are still on an older one. Shown to a super admin or a curriculum admin. A draft in progress is called out in Needs attention.

Needs attention links each item to the screen that deals with it:

| Waiting | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| Schools past due | Payments |
| Schools still pending | Directory filtered to pending (`/schools?status=pending`) |
| Schools on an older curriculum, or a draft waiting to publish | Curriculum |
| Failed sends, or sends with no price on record | Messages |
| A provider whose last sync failed, or one that is not configured | Providers |

## Platform admin — school detail

A **Messaging report** link sits next to Back to schools and opens `/messages/schools/:id`. Roles that cannot open Messages do not see it. The report already links back to the school record.

## Platform admin — free and sponsored

`GET /platform/messages/free-usage/` now includes `budget_ghs`, `budget_used_pct`, and `by_school`. The existing tiles and table read those fields.

- **Budget cap** is the sum of budgets on platform-scope allowances that are active now. A school allowance keeps its budget on its own policy row.
- **Budget used** is the provider cost, in the selected range, of sends tied to those platform allowances, as a percent of the cap.
- **By school** lists each school with free or sponsored traffic in the range: free and sponsored SMS segments, free and sponsored email credits, and the provider cost the platform absorbed. Paid sends are left out.
