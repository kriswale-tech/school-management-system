# 15 November 2026

Corrected remaining list. Onboarding a school that already has pupils is what is next. Bulk actions on the student list are parked until a school asks for them.

## Done today

Graduated students and students who have left can be found. The Students page opens on active students. Search by name or student ID also returns someone who has left or graduated. **Graduated** and **Left** are their own filters. Badges show on the list and on the profile. **Mark as left**, **Reactivate**, and **Clear graduation** sit on the student row, opposite the name. Bio keeps the leave reason, date, and notes.

A separate Graduate button, and bulk mark-left / graduate / reactivate, stay parked.

## Next — onboarding

Creating an empty school is already in place: platform admin creates it, invites the admin, they finish setup, then add the current term’s students one by one or from Excel. That Excel import creates current students only. An existing student ID is skipped. It does not load previous years, marks, or payments.

The open question is a school that already has pupils. Two ways:

- **This term only.** Use the setup wizard and the current Excel import. Old marks and old debt stay outside the app. From this term on, the app is the record.
- **Bring the past.** Previous years, the classes those pupils were in, fees already billed, and payments already taken, including who still owes. That import is in the app as of 20 November. Marks are still not imported. See [2026-11-20.md](./2026-11-20.md) and [migration.md](./migration.md).
