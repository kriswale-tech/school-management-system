## Platform admin — school directory and billing

The school list and detail page are described in [platform-admin-schools.md](./platform-admin-schools.md). What changed:

- The directory shows plan, renewal, admin, overdue, and staff and student counts, with a platform-wide summary strip.
- Changing a school’s status always asks for a reason. The reason is stored on the audit entry.
- School detail uses status chips, a large staff and student count, an admins table, and an invite modal. The audit log stays closed until it is opened.
- Subscription shows plan, interval, capacity bars, and the active discount (cancel or switch). Changing plan can wait until renewal, or a higher plan can be applied today. Applying today records the prorated invoice as paid and writes an audit entry. Adding credits from this screen does the same: paid, granted, and logged. The school is not sent through Paystack.
- Invoices and payments are separate tabs, ten rows per page, on the school detail page and on the platform payments page. Void invoices stay in the table and are left out of billed and discount totals. A pending Paystack row can be verified; an unconfirmed check leaves it unchanged.
- When a renewal is unpaid, Extend grace moves that school’s grace end later. The platform default still applies to every other school.
- Dates use the same readable formatting as the school app.

Discounts, finished with this work, apply to billing months rather than a whole invoice. A “first six months” offer on an annual invoice reduces half of the percent. The larger cedi saving wins when more than one discount matches. An introductory window starts from the school’s first subscription period, not from the payment interval.

## School app — Paystack checkout

School admins pay an open subscription invoice or buy communication credits from Settings → Billing and Settings → Messaging.

- Checkout opens the Paystack modal with `resumeTransaction(access_code)`. The amount and reference stay on the server. The public key is not required.
- If Paystack sends the payer out to mobile money or a bank and they return with `?payment=return`, the app verifies that reference. The webhook remains the source of truth.
- Paystack should call `POST /api/payments/paystack/webhook/`. The host is whatever is configured in the Paystack dashboard. The existing `/api/v1/billing/paystack/webhook/` route still works. The signature is HMAC-SHA512. A blank `PAYSTACK_SAAS_WEBHOOK_SECRET` falls back to `PAYSTACK_SAAS_SECRET_KEY`.
- A pending Billing row has **Confirm payment**. That calls verify again. Paystack success completes the charge. Anything else is left as Paystack reported it.
- A credit top-up is one invoice plus one top-up payment. Billing no longer offers Pay now on that invoice, and the school payment list hides the extra invoice-purpose row. Starting checkout on a top-up invoice is rejected. A later top-up is a new charge.

The verify lock uses `select_for_update(of=('self',))`. Locking the payment together with its nullable invoice fails on Postgres.

## School app — messaging credits

Settings → Messaging overview charts SMS and email separately: sent, failed, and skipped, plus credits used by event. The range is the last 7 days, the last 30 days, or a custom start and end of up to one year.

History can resend failed or skipped SMS and email that still have the original message. Unknown SMS must be verified before they can be resent. Delivered and submitted rows are not eligible.

- Checkboxes select rows on the current page, and that selection stays when the page changes.
- **Select eligible on this page** checks this page only.
- **Select all N eligible** checks every failed or skipped message that matches the current channel, status, type, and search. More than 100 uses the latest 100. The confirm step shows the SMS and email credits that set needs.
- A send that does not fit the balance sends what it can and leaves the rest skipped. Debt-reminder and report-ready previews warn before the staff member confirms. When a send actually skips for lack of credits, active admins and staff get an in-app notification (`messaging_credits`) that opens History filtered to those skipped messages. The person who sent is included.

Changing a history filter clears the selection.
